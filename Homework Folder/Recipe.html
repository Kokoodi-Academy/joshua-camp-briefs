Classic Tomato Pasta

Simple, fresh and delicious 🍝

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic, sliced
  • Tomatoes
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh basil
  • Parmesan cheese

Method

  1. Drop the pasta into boiling salted water and cook until al dente.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat.
  3. Add the sliced garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, until fragrant. Do not let it brown.
  4. Pour in the tomatoes and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Simmer the sauce gently for 10 minutes, until it has thickened.
  6. Before draining the pasta, reserve a small cup of the starchy pasta water.
  7. Add the drained pasta directly to the tomato sauce and toss well.
  8. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of pasta water and toss again.
  9. Turn off the heat. Tear the fresh basil by hand and stir it through the pasta.
  10. Serve warm with a generous grating of Parmesan.

Enjoy your homemade pasta! ❤️
