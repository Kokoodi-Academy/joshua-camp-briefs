Pepperoni Pizza

A crispy homemade pizza topped with tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and savory pepperoni.

Recipe Information

  • Preparation time: 20 minutes
  • Cooking time: 12-15 minutes
  • Servings: 2-3 people

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 20-25 pepperoni slices
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
  • Flour for dusting

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 230°C (450°F).
  2. Dust a baking sheet or pizza pan with flour.
  3. Stretch the dough into a round pizza base.
  4. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough.
  5. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  6. Arrange the pepperoni slices on top.
  7. Add olive oil, oregano, and garlic powder.
  8. Bake for 12-15 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  9. Let the pizza cool, then slice and serve.
